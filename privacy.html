Privacy Policy

A simple starter policy for this independent publication.

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Information We Receive

When you submit an article, the submission form may collect your name, email address, author bio, article title, category, article text and source information. This information is used to review, communicate about and manage submissions.

How We Use It

Submission information is used for editorial review, contributor communication and publication management. We do not ask contributors to submit passwords, payment-card information or government identification.

Third-Party Services

The starter version of this website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Sheets / Google Apps Script for article submissions. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Retention

Submission records may be kept for editorial history and administration. You may contact the site owner to request correction of contributor information that you previously submitted.

Contact

Replace the contact email on the Contact page with your current editorial email before publishing the website.
