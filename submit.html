Before submitting: Please send original work, use accurate references, avoid plagiarism, and do not include passwords, private documents or other sensitive information. Submission does not guarantee publication.

Submission Rules

  • Write on one of our listed categories.
  • Use your own words. Quote and cite sources properly.
  • For Qur'an and Hadith quotations, provide enough source detail for checking.
  • Do not submit abusive, threatening, deliberately misleading or sectarian attack content.
  • Use a clear title and readable structure with headings where useful.
  • We may edit grammar, formatting, headings and length while preserving the core argument.
Used only for editorial communication.
