About The Muslim Thinker

A small independent platform for careful Islamic writing and discussion.

Our Purpose

The Muslim Thinker is an educational publication focused on Islamic thought, Qur'an and Hadith, Islamic history, Muslim civilization, society, contemporary issues, research and book reviews.

Our aim is not simply to publish more content. We want to encourage careful reading, responsible argument, accurate sourcing and respectful intellectual discussion.

Editorial Principles

  • We prefer original writing over copied or recycled content.
  • Religious quotations should be checked and referenced carefully.
  • Authors should distinguish established facts from interpretation or opinion.
  • We do not publish abusive, threatening or deliberately misleading material.
  • Submission does not guarantee publication.

Who Can Contribute?

Students, researchers, teachers, writers and interested readers may submit suitable original work. Contributors do not need to agree with every position on this website, but submissions must remain respectful and evidence-based.
